What good looks like, in practice

We're a young advisory practice, so the examples below are illustrative — built from the kind of engagements we run, not published client case studies. Real, named case studies will replace these as clients agree to be featured.

Illustrative example

ITGC Health Check — retail SME

A 40-seat retailer with no documented change-management process for its point-of-sale system. A focused ITGC review mapped access, change and backup controls and closed the gaps auditors flag most often.

Zero ITGC deficiencies
at year-end audit, following remediation
Illustrative example

Internal controls setup — growing services firm

A business scaling past 25 staff with no segregation of duties in its payment process. Controls were mapped, documented and sized to the team — not a Big Four audit file.

4 weeks
from scoping call to signed-off controls framework
Illustrative example

ERP migration assurance — manufacturer

Independent pre- and post-implementation review of a finance system migration, catching a data-mapping error before go-live that would have misstated opening balances.

1 critical issue
caught before go-live, at no cost to the go-live date
